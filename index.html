FAQ

A few helpful answers.

Do I need an account?

No. Core tracking works without a DAWR account. An external provider account is only needed if you choose its optional backup service.

Where is my data stored?

Core tracking data is stored locally on your device. Optional backups are created only when you choose a backup method.

Can I track missed Ramadan fasts?

Yes. You can record missed Ramadan fasts and the make-up fasts you complete.

Does DAWR support pregnancy and postpartum?

Yes. You can select pregnancy or postpartum / nifas so cycle prediction behavior reflects that life stage.

Is the guidance medical or scholarly advice?

No. DAWR is a personal tracking and educational tool. It does not diagnose, treat, issue personal religious rulings or replace qualified care and consultation.
